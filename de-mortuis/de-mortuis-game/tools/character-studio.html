How to make a good character with a mouse

  1. Start from something: open your sketch, or pick a mannequin pose and trace it.
  2. Ink on the Lines layer with the pen. Stabiliser around 30–50, taper on. Draw long confident strokes; undo and redo a line rather than patching it.
  3. Use symmetry for faces and front-facing outfits, then turn it off for hair and poses.
  4. Flat colours on the Colour layer: bucket fill inside your lines (it ignores small gaps), or lasso big shapes.
  5. Auto shading once, then erase or lasso-cut bits you don't want on the Shading layer.
  6. Airbrush a little blush on cheeks, glow in the eyes, on the Light layer.
