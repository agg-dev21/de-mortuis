DE MORTUIS - CHARACTER SPRITES
==============================

DONE: all sprites are in SPRITES/ and wired into the game:
  iris-normal / iris-flustered / iris-depressed
  naomi-normal / naomi-flustered / naomi-depressed
  maeve-normal (smirk) / maeve-surprised
  (Maeve has two: there is no depressed Maeve, her smirk covers her Death side.)

Original brief for the artist below.

HOW TO DRAW THEM
  - Same outfits, hair and colours as the design sheets in characters/refs/.
  - Standing, facing the viewer (slightly turned is fine). Waist-up or full body.
  - Use the SAME pose for all three expressions of a girl, so only the face
    (and maybe shoulders/arms) changes. Then they can swap mid-scene without jumping.
  - Transparent background, PNG, about 1500 px tall, same canvas size for all three.
  - File names:
        iris-normal.png    iris-flustered.png    iris-depressed.png
        naomi-normal.png   naomi-flustered.png   naomi-depressed.png
        maeve-normal.png   maeve-flustered.png   maeve-depressed.png
  - Put them in characters/ (or send them over) and they go into the game.


WHERE EACH EXPRESSION SHOWS (the game picks it automatically)

IRIS (long blue hair)
  normal      most scenes
  flustered   warm moments: looking at her drawing, the pillow, "Deal.", when you ask
              if she wants people to know
  depressed   whenever her meter is low (her room also turns grey then)

NAOMI (blonde twin tails, blue ribbons)
  normal      most scenes (arms crossed suits her)
  flustered   her tsundere moments: the coffees, "don't make it weird",
              and after you tell her the truth
  depressed   when her meter is low, or after she finds out it was her
              and you haven't told her the truth

MAEVE (white hair, black suit) - Death
  normal      smirk: everywhere else
  surprised   "Oh, (name)": the Day 6 bench, the 2:07 on the date, the good-ending reveal
