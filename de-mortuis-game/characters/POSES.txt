DE MORTUIS - CHARACTER POSES TO DRAW
====================================

HOW TO DRAW THEM
  - Same outfits, hair and colours as the design sheets in characters/refs/.
  - Waist-up is enough (legs are never shown in the game). Full body is fine if it's easier.
  - Facing the viewer, slightly turned is fine. Leave some space around the figure.
  - Transparent background, PNG, about 1500 px tall.
  - Draw each FACE on its own layer, so faces can be swapped onto the same body.
    Export: body without face + one PNG per face (same canvas size, lined up).
  - Name files like:  iris-pose1.png, iris-face-tired.png, naomi-pose2.png ...
  - Start with pose 1 for each girl + all her faces. That alone covers about 70% of the scenes.


IRIS (long blue hair) - quiet, hurting, slowly opening up
----------------------------------------------------------
  Pose 1  Standing, arms wrapped around herself, shoulders hunched, head a little down.
          (hallways, classroom, "thank you for this week")
  Pose 2  Sitting with knees pulled up to her chest, arms around her legs.
          (her room, the pink chair in the lounge, sitting on the dryer)
  Pose 3  Holding her sketchbook flat against her chest with both arms.
          (art hallway Day 3, drawing again Day 5, giving you her drawing Day 6)
  Pose 4  Looking down at her phone, held in both hands.

  Faces:  tired / blank            (used most)
          small shy smile
          about to cry
          crying                   (Day 7, the 988 call)
          calm and polite          (scary: Day 7 when she's very low, "already decided")
          surprised / caught off guard
